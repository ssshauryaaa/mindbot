Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Pyro AI ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Pyro AI chatbot application available at pyrobot-chat.vercel.app and via WhatsApp integration. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Pyro AI chatbot (via our web app or WhatsApp), we may process:

  • The text messages you send to the chatbot.
  • Your WhatsApp phone number (only used to deliver AI responses back to you).
  • Basic usage metadata such as timestamps and response times.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI responses to your queries using third-party language models (via OpenRouter AI).
  • To improve the quality and accuracy of responses over time.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or third parties.

3. Data Retention

Conversation messages are processed in real-time and are not permanently stored on our servers. Temporary in-memory conversation history may be held for the duration of an active session and is discarded when the session ends.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to power this application:

  • OpenRouter AI — for language model inference. Their privacy policy applies to data sent to their API.
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business API) — for message delivery. Meta's privacy policy governs message transmission via WhatsApp.
  • Vercel — for web hosting and serverless function execution.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. All communications between your device and our servers are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. API keys and credentials are stored securely as environment variables and are never exposed publicly.

6. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
